Privacy Policy – Lizardex Clips

Last updated: 27 September 2026

1. Who we are

Lizardex Clips ("the app") is a private tool that publishes short video clips to social media accounts owned by its operator. It has no public users and no sign-up.

2. Data we process

  • For accounts the operator connects (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram): the account identifier (for example TikTok open_id), display name, and OAuth access and refresh tokens.
  • For videos the app publishes: post IDs, links and public statistics (views, likes, comments).

The app does not collect data about viewers or other TikTok users.

3. How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the operator's clips and to measure their performance. It is stored locally on the operator's own computer, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties, except the platforms themselves when publishing.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the connection is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time in the platform's app settings (on TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions), after which the tokens stop working and are deleted. Deletion of any data can be requested at the contact address below.
